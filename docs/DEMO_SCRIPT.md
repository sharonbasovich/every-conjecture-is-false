# Demo video script (target 90 seconds)

Record at 1440×900 on the live demo in a fresh browser profile (or click **Reset progress** first). `docs/video/walkthrough.mp4` is a silent short capture of this same flow that you can use as B-roll or as a fallback.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|------|-----------|------------|
| 0:00–0:08 | Landing page: headline "Every conjecture here is ~~true~~ false." | "Every claim in this app is false. Your job is to prove it." |
| 0:08–0:20 | Click **Euler's prime generator**. Hover over the green evidence chips. | "n squared plus n plus 41. It's prime for n equals 0, 1, 2… all forty values up to 39. That's the kind of evidence that convinces people." |
| 0:20–0:30 | Type `10`, press **Break it**. Grey verdict: 151 is prime. | "Try 10. It's prime. The app checks every answer exactly, with no answer key." |
| 0:30–0:42 | Type `40`, press **Break it**. FALSE stamp slams, the reveal appears. | "Try 40. That's 41 squared. Broken. And it tells you why the pattern fooled you." |
| 0:42–0:55 | Click **Circle regions double**, enter `6`. | "Points on a circle: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 regions… then 31. Your brain wanted 32." |
| 0:55–1:15 | Click **The 52-digit liar**. Show hints 1–3, paste the number, press **Break it**. | "The boss: this gcd is 1 for every n you could brute-force in the lifetime of the universe. The first counterexample has 52 digits. The only way to find it is algebra." |
| 1:15–1:25 | Deck on the left with FALSE badges; progress meter. | "Twelve famous claims, 350 years of math history, from warm-up to boss." |
| 1:25–1:30 | Headline again. | "Examples are evidence, not proof. Every Conjecture Here Is False." |
